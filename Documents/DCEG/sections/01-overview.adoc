

== Overview

=== Preface

The Data Classification and Encoding Guide (DCEG) has been developed to
provide consistent and standardised instructions for encoding S-100
compliant Catalogue of Nautical Products (S-128) data. It forms Annex A
to the S-128 Product Specification and is published as a separate
document, so that encoding guidance can be maintained and reissued
without amending the Product Specification itself.

The purpose of the DCEG is to facilitate S-128 encoding to a standard
that meets IHO requirements for the proper display of Catalogue of
Nautical Products information in an ECDIS and in other electronic
charting displays. Whereas the Product Specification defines what the
S-128 data product is, and the Feature Catalogue defines the types from
which it may be constructed, this document explains how those types are
to be applied to real world circumstances. It describes how to encode
information that the modeller considers relevant to a Catalogue of
Nautical Products data product, and identifies the conventions that
must be observed so that datasets produced by different authorities can
be interpreted consistently.

The content of a dataset is at the discretion of the producing
authority, provided that the conventions described within this document
are followed. That discretion is deliberate: the range of products
catalogued, and the level of detail recorded for each of them, will
differ between authorities according to national practice and the
services offered. What must not differ is the manner in which a given
item of information is expressed once an authority has decided to
record it. A producing authority is a Hydrographic Office (HO), or
another organisation authorised by a government to produce definitive
nautical information.

This document must be read in conjunction with the S-128 Product
Specification and the S-128 Feature Catalogue. Where this document and
the Feature Catalogue appear to disagree on the permitted attributes,
values or multiplicities of a type, the Feature Catalogue is
authoritative, and the discrepancy must be reported to the IHO Nautical
Information Provision Working Group (NIPWG) in accordance with the
clause "Maintenance" below.

The entire S-100 Universal Hydrographic Data Model, including the S-128
Product Specification, is available at the following web site,
http://www.iho.int/[http://www.iho.int].

=== Terms, definitions and abbreviations

[heading="terms and definitions"]
==== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the terms and definitions given
below apply. They are reproduced here because each of them bears
directly on how features, attributes and geometry are to be encoded;
the wider vocabulary of hydrography is defined in IHO S-32, and the
terms particular to the data product itself are defined in the S-128
Product Specification.

===== aggregation

special form of {{association}} that specifies a whole-part relationship
between the aggregate (whole) and a component (see composition)

===== association

semantic relationship between two or more classifiers that specifies
connections among their instances

NOTE: A binary association is an association among exactly two classifiers
(including the possibility of an association from a classifier to
itself)

===== attribute

named property of an entity

NOTE: Describes the geometrical, topological, thematic, or other characteristic
of an entity

===== composition

a strong {{aggregation}}; if a container object is deleted then all
of its containee objects are deleted as well (that is, containee objects
cannot exist without the container object)

===== curve

1-dimensional geometric primitive, representing the continuous image
of a line

NOTE: The boundary of a curve is the set of points at either end of
the curve. If the curve is a cycle, the two ends are identical, and
the curve (if topologically closed) is considered to not have a boundary.
The first point is called the start point, and the last is the end
point. Connectivity of the curve is guaranteed by the "continuous
image of a line"

===== enumeration

a fixed list of valid identifiers of named literal values. Attributes
of an enumerated type may only take values from this list
(source: ISO 19136:2007, Geographic information -- Geography Markup
Language (GML))

===== feature

abstraction of real world phenomena

NOTE: A feature may occur as a type or an instance. The terms
"feature type" or "feature instance" should be used when only one
is meant

[example]
The feature instance named "Eiffel Tower" may be classified with other
phenomena into a feature type "tower"

===== geometric primitive

geometric object representing a single, connected, homogeneous element
of geometry

NOTE: Geometric primitives are non-decomposed objects that present
information about geometric configuration. They include points, curves,
surfaces

===== maximum display scale

the largest value of the ratio of the linear dimensions of features
of a dataset presented in the display and the actual dimensions of
the features represented (largest scale) of the scale range of the
dataset

===== minimum display scale

the smallest value of the ratio of the linear dimensions of features
of a dataset presented in the display and the actual dimensions of
the features represented (smallest scale) of the scale range of the
dataset

===== point

0-dimensional geometric primitive, representing a position.

NOTE: The boundary of a point is the empty set

===== pointset

a set of points in geometric space

===== skin of the earth

a subset of the geographic (geo) features that must create a complete
non-overlapping coverage of the area of data coverage of an ENC dataset

===== surface

connected 2-dimensional geometric primitive, representing the continuous
image of a region of a plane

NOTE: The boundary of a surface is the set of oriented, closed curves
that delineate the limits of the surface

=== Use of language

The obligation attaching to every statement in this document is carried
by a single verb, and that verb is used consistently throughout. Where a
sentence contains none of the three verbs below, it is descriptive and
places no obligation on the encoder. Within this document:

"Must" indicates a mandatory requirement, which has to be satisfied for
the encoding to conform to this document;

"Should" indicates a recommendation, that is, the process that is
recommended to be followed, but which is not mandatory;

"May" means "allowed to" or "could possibly", and is not mandatory.

The verb "shall" is not used in this document. Requirements expressed as
mandatory in this document derive either from the S-128 Product
Specification and Feature Catalogue, in which case the multiplicities
recorded in the tables of this document are the authoritative statement
of the obligation, or from the encoding conventions established here in
the interests of consistency between producing authorities.

=== Maintenance

Changes to the Data Classification and Encoding Guide must occur in
accordance with the S-128 CNP Product Specification. Proposals for
change are submitted to and coordinated by NIPWG, and new editions of
this document must be made available via the IHO web site.

Because this document is an Annex to the Product Specification rather
than part of it, an edition of the DCEG may be reissued to correct or
extend encoding guidance without a corresponding change to the Product
Specification or the Feature Catalogue. Encoders should therefore verify
that they hold the current edition of this document, and not rely on the
edition of the Product Specification alone as an indication of currency.
