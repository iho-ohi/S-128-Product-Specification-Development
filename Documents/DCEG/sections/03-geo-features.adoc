
== Geo Features

This clause describes the geographic feature types defined by S-128. Each
of them models a class of item that may be recorded in a Catalogue of
Nautical Products, and each is presented in the same tabular form so
that the permitted content of a feature instance can be read at a
glance.

The upper rows of every table identify the type: the IHO definition of
the real world concept it represents, the class name used in the Feature
Catalogue, the super type from which it inherits, and the geometric
primitives it may carry. The rows beneath list the attributes that may
be encoded, with the S-57 acronym where an equivalent existed in that
standard, the values permitted for enumerations, the attribute type, and
the multiplicity that determines whether the attribute is optional or
mandatory. Sub-attributes of a complex attribute are marked (S) and
follow the complex attribute to which they belong, as described in the
clause "Attributes". The lower rows record the INT 1 reference, any
remarks bearing on encoding, the distinction from types with which the
feature might be confused, and the associations in which instances of
the type may participate.

The five types described below form an inheritance hierarchy.
*CatalogueElement* is the root and holds the attributes common to
everything a catalogue may contain. *NavigationalProduct* specialises it
for items intended for navigation, and is in turn specialised by
*ElectronicProduct* and *PhysicalProduct* according to the form in which
the product is issued. *S100Service* specialises *CatalogueElement*
directly, for content delivered as a service rather than as a product.
Each table lists only the attributes introduced by the type it
describes; the attributes of every super type are inherited and may be
encoded on an instance of the subtype without being repeated here. An
encoder working on an *ElectronicProduct* instance must therefore read
three tables together — those of *CatalogueElement*, *NavigationalProduct*
and *ElectronicProduct* — to establish the full set of attributes
available and the obligations attaching to them.

=== Catalogue element

*CatalogueElement* is the root of the S-128 feature hierarchy and models
any item that a catalogue may contain, whether a product, a service or a
publication. Because every other geographic feature type in S-128
derives from it, the attributes listed below are available on every
feature instance in a dataset, and the conventions established here
apply throughout.

The table records the descriptive characteristics that are common to all
catalogued items: who is responsible for the content, how the item is
classified, what it is called, where further information about it may be
obtained, and from what source the entry was compiled. Two attributes
carry a mandatory obligation. *catalogue element classification* has
multiplicity 1,* and must be populated with at least one value from the
enumeration; more than one may be given where an item falls into several
classes. *not for navigation* has multiplicity 1,1 and must be populated
on every instance, since a consuming system cannot infer the navigational
status of an item that omits it. The remaining attributes are optional,
and the complex attributes *feature name*, *information*, *online
resource*, *source indication* and *support file* are each followed by
their sub-attributes, marked (S).

The associations listed in the table determine how an instance of
*CatalogueElement* is reached and what may be attached to it. Product
Mapping relates one catalogue element to another and is the means by
which a traditional product is associated with its S-100 equivalent.
Product Package relates the element to the *CatalogueSectionHeader* under
which it appears; its multiplicity of 1,1 at the *CatalogueElement* end
means that every catalogue element must belong to exactly one section,
and an element encoded without that association cannot be placed within
the catalogue. Carriage Requirement and Price Of Element are optional and
attach regulatory and pricing information respectively, each of which is
held as an information type so that a single instance may serve any
number of elements.

*CatalogueElement* is abstract in the sense that an encoder would not
normally create an instance of it directly. In practice a catalogued item
is encoded as one of its subtypes, and the attributes above are populated
on that instance.

=== Navigational product

*NavigationalProduct* specialises *CatalogueElement* for items that are
primarily intended for navigation. It adds the attributes by which one
edition of a chart or dataset is distinguished from another, and by which
a consuming system determines the scales at which the product is
intended to be used and the datums to which its content is referred.

The attributes below are additional to those inherited from
*CatalogueElement*, all of which remain available. Three groups warrant
attention. The scale attributes — *compilation scale*, *maximum display
scale*, *minimum display scale* and *optimum display scale* — are
distinct from one another and must not be used interchangeably;
compilation scale records the scale at which the data was compiled,
whereas the display scales govern the range over which it should be
presented. The identification attributes *edition number*, *update
number*, *update date* and *product number* together identify a
particular issue of a product, and should be populated as a set where
the product is one that is reissued. The *horizontal datum EPSG code* and
*vertical datum* attributes record the reference surfaces to which
positions and depths in the catalogued product are referred; they
describe the product being catalogued, not the S-128 dataset itself.

The Correlated association relates one *NavigationalProduct* to another
where a product carries panels, insets or similar subordinate
components. Both ends have multiplicity 0,*, so the association is
optional at either end and a product may carry several panels while a
panel may belong to more than one main product. The roles are not
interchangeable: *theMain* must be taken by the product that contains the
panel, and *thePanel* by the component it contains.

Like *CatalogueElement*, *NavigationalProduct* would not normally be
instantiated directly. A product intended for navigation is encoded as
either an *ElectronicProduct* or a *PhysicalProduct*, described in the two
clauses that follow, according to the form in which it is issued.

=== Electronic product

*ElectronicProduct* specialises *NavigationalProduct* for products issued
in digital form. It adds the attributes needed to identify the format in
which the product is encoded, the specification to which it conforms and
the moment at which the particular issue was released. It is the
counterpart of *PhysicalProduct*, which is described in the following
clause.

Two attributes are mandatory. *issue date* has multiplicity 1,1 and must
be populated on every instance, and *type of product format* has
multiplicity 1,1 and must be populated with one of the enumerated
values, since a consuming system cannot process a digital product whose
format is undeclared. The complex attribute *product specification* is
optional, but where it is encoded its sub-attributes *edition date*,
*name* and *version* each have multiplicity 1,1 and must be populated;
*ISSN* remains optional within it. Where a product is issued in more
than one format, each format must be encoded as a separate feature
instance rather than by repeating *type of product format*, whose upper
multiplicity is 1.

*ElectronicProduct* declares no associations of its own. Those available
to an instance are the ones inherited from *NavigationalProduct* and
*CatalogueElement*, in particular Product Package, which must be
populated for the instance to appear within a catalogue section.

=== Physical product

*PhysicalProduct* specialises *NavigationalProduct* for products issued
on paper. It adds the attributes describing the printed artefact itself
— when it was published, how it is identified in print, at what size it
is produced and by whom it was printed — none of which have meaning for
a product issued digitally.

*edition date* and *publication date* both have multiplicity 1,1 and
must be populated. The complex attribute *print size* has multiplicity
1,1 within *print information* and must therefore be populated wherever
*print information* is encoded; within it, a standard size is recorded
using *ISO 216*, and *custom paper size* is used only where the sheet
does not correspond to a standard size, in which case *paper length* and
*paper width* are each mandatory. The two should not both be encoded for
the same sheet. *reference to NM* records the Notice to Mariners by
which the product was last corrected, using the sub-attribute complex
*week of year*, whose *week number* and *year number* are each mandatory
when it is encoded.

=== S100 service

*S100Service* specialises *CatalogueElement* for content delivered as a
service rather than as a discrete product. A service differs from a
product in that it is not issued in editions but is made available
continuously, and its entry in the catalogue therefore records its
operational status and the specifications governing it rather than an
edition and update history.

*type of product format* has multiplicity 1,1 and must be populated.
*service status* is optional but should be populated wherever it is
known, since a consuming system has no other means of determining
whether a listed service is provisional, released, deprecated or
deleted, and may otherwise present a withdrawn service as available. The
table distinguishes two complex attributes that are easily confused:
*service specification* identifies the specification governing the
service itself, whereas *product specification* identifies the
specification governing the data the service delivers. Where both apply
they must both be encoded, and each carries the mandatory sub-attributes
*edition date*, *name* and *version*.

*S100Service* declares no associations of its own, and an instance

This completes the geographic feature types defined by S-128. The
information types that may be associated with them are described in the
clause that follows.
