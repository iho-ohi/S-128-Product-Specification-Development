
== Information types

This clause describes the information types defined by S-128. An
information type carries attributes but no geometry, and is reached only
through an association with a feature or with another information type,
as described in the clause "Information types" above. Its purpose is to
hold content that applies to more than one catalogued item, so that the
content is encoded once and referenced wherever it applies.

The tables follow the same form as those in the preceding clause, except
that the row headed [underline]#Primitives:# records None throughout,
since no information type may carry geometry. An information instance
that is not the target of at least one association cannot be reached by a
consuming system and must not be encoded; the associations available to
each type are listed in the lower rows of its table, and are described in
full in the clauses "Association Names" and "Association Roles".

The six types below fall into three groups. *CatalogueSectionHeader*
structures the catalogue itself, dividing it into the sections under
which catalogue elements are presented. *ProducerInformation*,
*DistributorInformation* and *ContactDetails* identify the organisations
associated with the catalogued items and record how they may be reached.
*PriceInformation* and *IndicationOfCarriageRequirement* carry commercial
and regulatory information attaching to catalogue elements. None of the
information types participates in an inheritance hierarchy, so each
table below is complete in itself and no attributes are inherited from
elsewhere.

=== Catalogue section header

*CatalogueSectionHeader* models the division of a catalogue into
sections, and is the means by which catalogue elements are grouped and
ordered for presentation. It is the structural backbone of an S-128
dataset: every catalogue element is placed within a section by the
Product Package association, and a dataset therefore requires at least
one instance of this type.

*catalogue section number* has multiplicity 1,1 and must be populated. It
determines the position of the section within the catalogue, and the
values encoded across a dataset should form a coherent sequence, since a
consuming system has no other basis on which to order the sections.
*catalogue section title* is optional but should be populated wherever
the section is intended to be presented to a user under a heading. The
complex attribute *information* may be used to carry an explanatory note
about the section as a whole, subject to the conventions in the clause
"Textual information".

The associations listed above establish the four relationships in which a
section header participates. Product Package attaches the catalogue
elements presented within the section. Production Details and
Distribution Details identify the organisations responsible for producing
and distributing the content of the section, and Price Of Nautical
Product attaches pricing that applies to the section as a whole rather
than to an individual element.

Pricing may therefore be encoded at either of two levels. Where a price
applies to every element within a section it should be attached to the
section header, and where prices differ between elements they must be
attached to the individual elements by the Price Of Element association
described under *CatalogueElement*.

=== Contact details

*ContactDetails* models the means by which a person or organisation may
be reached, and is used by both *ProducerInformation* and
*DistributorInformation* rather than being attached to catalogue elements
directly. Because a single organisation commonly acts in more than one
capacity, encoding contact information as a separate information type
allows one instance to serve several producers or distributors without
repetition.

*contact instructions* has multiplicity 1,1 and must be populated on
every instance. The remaining attributes are optional and are organised
into three complex attributes, each of which may be repeated: *contact
address* for postal contact, *online resource* for contact through a
network service, and *telecommunications* for voice, facsimile,
electronic mail and similar channels. Within *telecommunications*, the
sub-attribute *telecommunication service* identifies the channel to which
a *telecommunication identifier* applies; an identifier encoded without
its service cannot be interpreted, and the two should therefore be
populated together. Where an organisation may be reached by several
channels, each is encoded as a separate instance of *telecommunications*
rather than by combining them in a single value.

distinct instance of this type.

=== Indication of carriage requirement

*IndicationOfCarriageRequirement* records the regulatory basis on which a
catalogued product is required to be carried, whether under SOLAS, under
another international instrument, or under national legislation. It is
attached to catalogue elements by the Carriage Requirement association,
whose multiplicity permits a single instance to serve every element to
which the same requirement applies.

Both *domestic carriage requirements* and *international carriage
requirements* are optional free text attributes, and are distinguished by
the jurisdiction from which the requirement derives rather than by the
waters in which it applies. Where a product is subject to requirements of
both kinds, both attributes should be populated on the same instance. As
free text attributes their content cannot be interpreted by a consuming
system, and the conventions in the clause "Textual information"
concerning language apply to them.

confine the content of both attributes to a plain statement of the
requirement and the instrument from which it derives, and should not
attempt to encode conditions or exemptions that a consuming system would
be unable to interpret.

*IndicationOfCarriageRequirement* declares no associations of its own in
the table above; it is reached from *CatalogueElement* by the Carriage
Requirement association, which is described in the clause "Association
Names".

=== Price information

*PriceInformation* records the cost of obtaining a catalogued item. It is
encoded as an information type so that a single price may be attached
either to an individual catalogue element, by the Price Of Element
association, or to a catalogue section as a whole, by the Price Of
Nautical Product association.

The commercial content is carried by the complex attribute *pricing*,
which may be repeated. Within a single instance of *pricing*, both
*currency* and *price* have multiplicity 1,1 and must be populated; a
price encoded without its currency cannot be interpreted, and the
currency must therefore be given even where a producing authority
publishes in one currency only. *contract period* is optional and
distinguishes prices that apply for a stated term. Where an item is
offered at different prices under different terms, each is encoded as a
separate instance of *pricing* rather than by expressing the variation as
free text.

attach a price at one level only for any given item.

=== Producer information

*ProducerInformation* identifies the organisation responsible for
producing a catalogued item. It is attached to a catalogue section by the
Production Details association, and is linked to the means of contacting
the organisation by the Production Contact association, which references
an instance of *ContactDetails*.

Both attributes are simple text attributes, and the distinction between
them is set out in the remarks below the table. *agency responsible for
production* has multiplicity 1,1 and must be populated on every instance;
*agency name* is optional and is populated only where the organisation
carrying out production differs from the organisation responsible for the
content. Where the two are the same, *agency name* should be omitted
rather than populated with a duplicate value.

against the catalogue section whose content it produced. Both are
optional at the *ProducerInformation* end, but an instance that
participates in neither is unreachable within the dataset and serves no
purpose.

Where a catalogue contains products from several producing authorities,
a separate instance of this type is encoded for each, and each is
associated with the sections to which it applies.

=== Distributor information

*DistributorInformation* identifies an organisation through which a
catalogued item may be obtained. It is the counterpart of
*ProducerInformation*, and the two are deliberately separate: the
organisation that produces an item and the organisation that distributes
it are frequently distinct, and a single item may be available from
several distributors.

*distributor name* has multiplicity 1,1 and must be populated; it is the
only attribute the type declares. The means of contacting the
distributor is not encoded here but on an associated instance of
*ContactDetails*, reached by the Distributor Contact association, and an
instance of *DistributorInformation* encoded without that association
records a name that a user has no means of acting upon.

This completes the information types defined by S-128. The associations
referred to throughout this clause and the preceding one are described in
the two clauses that follow: "Association Names" defines each association
and the ends it connects, and "Association Roles" defines the role names
taken at those ends.
