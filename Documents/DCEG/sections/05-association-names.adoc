
== Association Names

An association is a semantic relationship between two types, and is the
mechanism by which the feature types and information types described in
the two preceding clauses are joined together to form a coherent
catalogue. Associations are not attributes: they are not populated with a
value but are established between two instances, and the meaning of the
relationship is carried by the association itself.

Every association has a name, which identifies the relationship, and two
ends, each of which has a role name identifying the part the instance at
that end plays in it. This clause defines the associations by name; the
role names taken at the ends are defined in the clause "Association
Roles" that follows. The two clauses are complementary and are read
together, since neither an association nor a role is meaningful without
the other.

Each table below records the IHO definition of the association, any
remarks bearing on its use, and the two ends. For each end, the table
gives the role type, the role name, the type with which that end is
associated, and the multiplicity. The multiplicity is read in the manner
described in the clause "Multiplicity": it constrains the number of
instances that may appear at that end of the relationship, and a lower
limit of 1 or more makes the association mandatory for an instance of the
type at the opposite end. The multiplicities recorded here correspond to
those shown in the association rows of the feature and information type
tables in the two preceding clauses, and the two must be read as a single
statement of the constraint.

Most associations carry no attributes of their own. Where an association
does carry attributes, as Product Mapping does, they are listed in a
further block beneath the association ends and are encoded on the
relationship rather than on either of the instances it joins.

=== Carriage requirement

Carriage Requirement relates a catalogue element to the regulatory
provision under which the product it describes is required to be carried.
It is the means by which a catalogue records that an item is not merely
available but mandated, whether by SOLAS or by another instrument.

[cols="4",options="unnumbered"]
|===
4+a|

[underline]#IHO Definition:# A carriage requirement required by SOLAS
or other regulation.

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

h| Role Type h| Role h| Associated With h| Multiplicity
.2+| Association | The Element     | *Catalogue Element*                  | 1,*
                 | The Requirement | *Indication Of Carriage Requirement* | 0,*

|===

The multiplicity of 1,* at the *Catalogue Element* end means that where
the association is established, it must reference at least one catalogue
element and may reference many, so a single carriage requirement may
apply across an entire class of products. The association is optional at
the requirement end, and the great majority of catalogue elements will
not participate in it.

=== Distribution details

Distribution Details relates a catalogue section to the organisation
through which the items in that section may be obtained. Distribution is
recorded at section level rather than against individual elements,
reflecting the common arrangement in which a distributor handles a whole
class of products.

[cols="4",options="unnumbered"]
|===
4+a|

[underline]#IHO Definition:# Details related to distribution

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

h| Role Type h| Role h| Associated With h| Multiplicity
.2+| Association | Catalogue Header | *Catalogue Section Header* | 0,*
| The Distributor | *Distributor Information* | 0,*

|===

Both ends permit multiple instances, so a section may be served by
several distributors and a distributor may serve several sections. Where
an item is available from more than one source, this is expressed by
establishing the association once for each distributor rather than by
recording alternative sources as text.

=== Distributor contact

Distributor Contact relates a distributor to the means by which it may be
reached. It is the counterpart of Producer Contact, and exists separately
because contact details are held as an independent information type that
may be shared between organisations.

[cols="4",options="unnumbered"]
|===
4+a|

[underline]#IHO Definition:# Contact information of distributor

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

h| Role Type h| Role h| Associated With h| Multiplicity
.2+| Association | The Distributor | *Distributor Information* | 0,1
| The Contact Details | *Contact Details* | 0,*

|===

The multiplicity of 0,1 at the distributor end and 0,* at the contact
details end means that one distributor may hold several sets of contact
details, but a given set of contact details is attributed to at most one
distributor. Where two organisations genuinely share contact
arrangements, separate instances of *Contact Details* must be encoded.

=== Product mapping

Product Mapping relates one catalogue element to another where the two
describe equivalent or complementary content, and is the mechanism by
which a traditional product is associated with its S-100 successor. Both
ends are taken by *Catalogue Element*, so the association is reflexive:
it joins two instances of the same type rather than two different types.

Unlike the other associations in this clause, Product Mapping carries an
attribute. *category of product mapping* has multiplicity 1,1 and must be
populated whenever the association is established, since the
relationship is directional and its meaning depends entirely on the
value given. The value is encoded on the association itself and not on
either of the catalogue elements it joins.

[cols="5",options="unnumbered"]
|===
5+a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Mapping between traditional products
and S-100 Products.

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

h| Role Type h| Role 2+h| Associated With h| Multiplicity
.2+| Association | The Source    2+| *Catalogue Element* | 1,1
                 | The Reference 2+| *Catalogue Element* | 0,*

h| S-128 Attribute h| S-57 Acronym h| Allowable Encoding Value h| Type h| Multiplicity

| category of product mapping |
| 1: higher priority alternative +
2: lower priority alternative +
3: recommended enhancement provider +
4: recommended enhancement user
| EN | 1,1

|===

The two roles must not be interchanged. *The Source* is taken by the
element from which the mapping is made and *The Reference* by the element
to which it points, and reversing them inverts the meaning of *category
of product mapping*. The multiplicity of 1,1 at the source end and 0,* at
the reference end permits one element to point to several others, which
is the arrangement required where a single traditional chart is
superseded by more than one S-100 product.

=== Price of element

Price Of Element relates an individual catalogue element to the price at
which it may be obtained. It is one of the two associations by which
pricing may be attached, the other being Price Of Nautical Product,
described in the clause that follows.

[cols="4",options="unnumbered"]
|===
4+a|

[underline]#IHO Definition:# An association of price information to
a catalogue element

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

h| Role Type h| Role h| Associated With h| Multiplicity
.2+| Association | The Catalogue Element | *Catalogue Element* | 0,1
                 | The Price Information | *Price Information* | 0,*

|===

The multiplicity of 0,1 at the *Catalogue Element* end and 0,* at the
*Price Information* end means that an element carries at most one price
through this association, while a single price instance may be shared by
any number of elements. Where the price of an element changes according
to the terms of supply, the variation is expressed within the *pricing*
complex attribute of the price instance and not by establishing this
association more than once.

=== Price of nautical product

Price Of Nautical Product relates a catalogue section to a price applying
to the section as a whole. It is used where a single price covers every
item in a section, in preference to attaching the same price
individually to each element by Price Of Element.

[cols="4",options="unnumbered"]
|===
4+a|
[underline]#IHO Definition:# The price of a nautical product

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

h| Role Type h| Role h| Associated With h| Multiplicity
.2+| Association | The Catalogue Of Nautical Product | *Catalogue Section Header* | 1,1
                 | The Price Information             | *Price Information*        | 0,*

|===

Where prices are attached at both section and element level for the same
item, the two associations overlap and the resulting entry is ambiguous.
Encoders should attach a price at one level only for any given item.

NOTE: [NIPWG REVIEW REQUIRED] The multiplicity recorded at the
*Catalogue Section Header* end of this association is 1,1 in the table
below, but 0,* in the association rows of the *PriceInformation* table in
the clause "Information types". The two statements are inconsistent and
the intended constraint requires confirmation.

=== Producer contact

Producer Contact relates a producing organisation to the means by which
it may be reached. Contact details are held separately from
*Producer Information* so that an organisation acting as both producer
and distributor need be described only once.

[cols="4",options="unnumbered"]
|===
4+a|
[underline]#IHO Definition:# Contact information of producer

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

h| Role Type h| Role h| Associated With h| Multiplicity
.2+| Association | The Producer        | *Producer Information* | 0,1
                 | The Contact Details | *Contact Details*      | 0,*
|===

As with Distributor Contact, the multiplicity of 0,1 at the producer end
means that a set of contact details is attributed to at most one
producer. An organisation acting in both capacities is therefore
associated with its contact details twice, once through this association
and once through Distributor Contact.

=== Production details

Production Details relates a catalogue section to the organisation
responsible for producing the items within it. Where a catalogue draws
together products from several producing authorities, the association is
established for each of them against the sections to which it applies.

[cols="4",options="unnumbered"]
|===
4+a|
[underline]#IHO Definition:# Contact information of a producing organization

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

h| Role Type h| Role h| Associated With h| Multiplicity
.2+| Association | Catalogue Header | *Catalogue Section Header* | 0,1
                 | The Producer     | *Producer Information*     | 0,*
|===

The association is optional at both ends, but a catalogue section whose
producer is not identified gives the user no basis on which to judge the
authority of its content. The association should therefore be
established for every section wherever the producing organisation is
known.

=== Product package

Product Package relates a catalogue element to the section under which it
is presented, and is the association that gives a catalogue its
structure. Of all the associations defined in this clause it is the one
most likely to be overlooked, since an element may be fully populated
with attributes and still be absent from the catalogue if this
association is not established.

[cols="4",options="unnumbered"]
|===
4+a|
[underline]#IHO Definition:# A package or distinct set of products

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

h| Role Type h| Role h| Associated With h| Multiplicity
.2+| Association | The Catalogue Element | *Catalogue Element*        | 1,1
                 | Element Container     | *Catalogue Section Header* | 1,*
|===

The multiplicity of 1,1 at the *Catalogue Element* end is the strongest
constraint in the S-128 model. Every catalogue element must belong to
exactly one section, and an element that does not participate in this
association cannot be presented to a user however completely its
attributes are populated. The multiplicity of 1,* at the section end
means that a section must contain at least one element; an empty section
must not be encoded.

=== Correlated

Correlated relates one navigational product to another where the second
forms a subordinate part of the first, such as a panel or inset carried
on a chart. Both ends are taken by *Navigational Product*, making the
association reflexive in the same manner as Product Mapping.

[cols="4",options="unnumbered"]
|===
4+a|
[underline]#IHO Definition:# A supplementary or secondary part of
the product, which may appear multiple times, offering control or
display functionalities depending on its configuration

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

h| Role Type h| Role h| Associated With h| Multiplicity
.2+| Association | The Main  | *Navigational Product* | 0,*
                 | The Panel | *Navigational Product* | 0,*
|===

Both ends have multiplicity 0,*, so a main product may carry several
panels and a panel may be shared between main products. As with Product
Mapping, the roles are not interchangeable: *The Main* must be taken by
the containing product and *The Panel* by the component it contains.

This completes the associations defined by S-128. The role names taken at
the ends of these associations are defined in the clause that follows.
