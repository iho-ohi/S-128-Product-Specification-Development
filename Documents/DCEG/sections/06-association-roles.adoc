
== Association Roles

Each end of an association carries a role name, which identifies the part
played by the instance at that end. The role name is what distinguishes
the two ends of a relationship from one another, and it is therefore
essential wherever an association is directional or reflexive: in
Product Mapping and in Correlated, both ends are taken by the same type,
and only the role names record which instance is the source and which the
reference, or which is the main product and which the panel.

A role name is not encoded as an attribute value. It is a property of the
association, and identifies the end at which an instance is attached.
The default naming rule given in the S-128 Product Specification applies
throughout: unless an explicit role name is shown, the role is named
'the<CLASSNAME>', where <CLASSNAME> is the name of the class to which
that end is linked. The role names defined below follow that rule with
the exception of *Catalogue Header* and *Element Container*, which are
named for the part they play rather than for the class at the end.

The clauses below define each role name used in S-128 and, for each,
identify the associations in which it appears. Role names are presented
here in the spaced and capitalised form used for headings; in the
association rows of the feature and information type tables in the
clauses "Geo Features" and "Information types" the same names appear in
the concatenated form used by the Feature Catalogue, so that *The
Catalogue Element* below and theCatalogueElement in those tables denote
one and the same role. The associations themselves are defined in the
clause "Association Names".

=== Catalogue header

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# The top section of a catalogue.

|===

*Catalogue Header* is taken by the *Catalogue Section Header* end of the
Production Details and Distribution Details associations. It identifies
the section of the catalogue whose content a given producer or
distributor is responsible for.

=== Element container

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# A container of elements.

|===

*Element Container* is taken by the *Catalogue Section Header* end of the
Product Package association. It identifies the section within which a
catalogue element is presented, and is named for the containing function
it performs rather than for the class at that end.

=== The catalogue element

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Reference to an element within a catalogue.

|===

*The Catalogue Element* is taken by the *Catalogue Element* end of the
Product Package and Price Of Element associations. It identifies the
element that is placed within a section, or to which a price applies.

=== The catalogue of nautical product

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Reference to a Catalogue of Nautical product.

|===

*The Catalogue Of Nautical Product* is taken by the *Catalogue Section
Header* end of the Price Of Nautical Product association. It identifies
the section to which a price applies where that price covers the section
as a whole rather than an individual element.

=== The contact details

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Information on how to reach a person
or organisation by postal, internet, telephone, telex and radio systems.

|===

*The Contact Details* is taken by the *Contact Details* end of the
Producer Contact and Distributor Contact associations. It identifies the
means by which the organisation at the opposite end of the association
may be reached.

=== The distributor

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Reference to the distributor.

|===

*The Distributor* is taken by the *Distributor Information* end of the
Distribution Details and Distributor Contact associations. It identifies
the organisation through which the items in a catalogue section may be
obtained.

=== The element

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Reference to an element.

|===

*The Element* is taken by the *Catalogue Element* end of the Carriage
Requirement association. It identifies the catalogue element to which a
carriage requirement applies, and is distinct from *The Catalogue
Element*, which is used by the Product Package and Price Of Element
associations.

=== The price information

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Reference to price information.

|===

*The Price Information* is taken by the *Price Information* end of both
the Price Of Element and Price Of Nautical Product associations. It
identifies the price attaching to an element or to a section.

=== The producer

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Reference to a producer.

|===

*The Producer* is taken by the *Producer Information* end of the
Production Details and Producer Contact associations. It identifies the
organisation responsible for producing the content of a catalogue
section.

=== The reference

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Reference to supporting material or
information related to a specific element or data.

|===

*The Reference* is taken by one end of the Product Mapping association,
and identifies the catalogue element to which a mapping points. Because
both ends of that association are taken by *Catalogue Element*, this role
name and *The Source* are the only means of establishing the direction of
the relationship, and they must not be interchanged.

=== The requirement

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Reference to a requirement for a specific
system or process.

|===

*The Requirement* is taken by the *Indication Of Carriage Requirement*
end of the Carriage Requirement association. It identifies the regulatory
provision under which the associated catalogue elements are required to
be carried.

=== The source

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Reference to the source of information
or data.

|===

*The Source* is taken by one end of the Product Mapping association, and
identifies the catalogue element from which a mapping is made. It is the
counterpart of *The Reference*, and the value of *category of product
mapping* is to be read as describing the relationship of the source to
the reference and not the reverse.

=== The main

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Reference to the main product containing
panel(s).

|===

*The Main* is taken by one end of the Correlated association, and
identifies the navigational product that contains a panel. As with
Product Mapping, both ends of the Correlated association are taken by the
same type, so this role name and *The Panel* are the only means of
establishing which product contains the other.

=== The panel

[cols=1,options="unnumbered,noheader"]
|===
| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Reference to the panel of a main product.

|===

*The Panel* is taken by one end of the Correlated association, and
identifies the subordinate component, such as a panel or inset, carried
on the product at the opposite end. It is the counterpart of *The Main*
and must not be interchanged with it.

This completes the definition of the S-128 model. The clauses that follow
describe the individual attributes and their permitted values, and the
complex attributes constructed from them.
