
[[sec_8]]
== Complex Attributes

A complex attribute is an aggregate of other attributes, which may
themselves be simple or complex. It has no value of its own: it is a
container, and the information is carried by the sub-attributes bound to
it. This clause defines the complex attributes used by S-128 and lists
the sub-attributes belonging to each, with a cross-reference to the
definition of every sub-attribute in the clause "Attribute and Enumerate
Descriptions".

The sub-attributes listed here are those the complex attribute may
contain. Whether a given sub-attribute is optional or mandatory, and how
many instances of it are permitted, is not a property of the complex
attribute in isolation but of its binding to the type on which it is
encoded, and is recorded in the attribute rows of the tables in the
clauses "Geo Features" and "Information types". In those tables a complex
attribute is followed by its sub-attributes, each marked (S), and the
multiplicity shown against a sub-attribute applies within a single
instance of its parent. Where the parent complex attribute is not
encoded, no obligation attaching to its sub-attributes arises.

Two consequences follow for the encoder. A complex attribute must not be
encoded unless at least one of its sub-attributes is populated, since an
empty container conveys nothing. And where a complex attribute may be
repeated, each repetition is a self-contained set of sub-attribute
values: information belonging to one occurrence must not be distributed
across several, nor several occurrences combined into one.

Each entry below records the IHO definition of the complex attribute, the
sub-attributes it comprises, and any remarks bearing on its use, followed
by a note of the types on which it is encoded.

[[sec_8.1]]
=== contact address

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Direction or superscription of a letter,
package, etc., specifying the name of the place to which it is directed,
and optionally a contact person or organisation who should receive
it.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*administrative division* (see <<sec_7.1>>) +
*city name* (see <<sec_7.13>>) +
*country name* (see <<sec_7.20>>) +
*delivery point* (see <<sec_7.25>>) +
*postal code* (see <<sec_7.61>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*contact address* is encoded on *ContactDetails*, where it carries the
postal contact for a producer or distributor. Where an organisation has
more than one address, each is encoded as a separate instance.

[[sec_8.2]]
=== custom paper size

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# User specified paper size width x,
height y.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*paper length* (see <<sec_7.59>>) +
*paper width* (see <<sec_7.60>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*custom paper size* is encoded within *print information* on
*PhysicalProduct*. It is used only where the sheet does not correspond to
a standard size; where it does, the size is recorded using *ISO 216*
instead, and the two should not both be encoded for the same sheet.

[[sec_8.3]]
=== default locale

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Locale of an option that is selected
automatically unless an alternative is specified.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*character encoding* (see <<sec_7.12>>) +
*country name* (see <<sec_7.20>>) +
*language* (see <<sec_7.46>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*default locale* is encoded on *CatalogueElement* and is therefore
available on every feature type in the model. It records the language and
character encoding that apply to the textual content of the catalogued
item in the absence of any more specific indication.

[[sec_8.4]]
=== feature name

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Provides the name of an entity, defines
the national language of the name, and provides the option to display
the name at various system display settings.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*language* (see <<sec_7.46>>) +
*name* (see <<sec_7.50>>) +
*name usage* (see <<sec_7.52>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*feature name* is encoded on *CatalogueElement*, and consequently on
every type deriving from it, as well as on *ContactDetails*,
*IndicationOfCarriageRequirement* and *PriceInformation*. It may be
repeated, which is how a name is provided in more than one language; the
sub-attribute *name usage* governs which of the names encoded is
intended for display, as described in the clause "Attribute and Enumerate
Descriptions".

[[sec_8.5]]
=== information

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Textual information about the feature.
The information may be provided as a string of text or as a file name
of a single external text file that contains the text.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*file locator* (see <<sec_7.34>>) +
*file reference* (see <<sec_7.36>>) +
*headline* (see <<sec_7.37>>) +
*language* (see <<sec_7.46>>) +
*text* (see <<sec_7.85>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*information* is encoded on *CatalogueElement*, *CatalogueSectionHeader*,
*ContactDetails* and *PriceInformation*. It is the general mechanism for
carrying explanatory text, and its use is governed by the clause
"Textual information", which limits the sub-attribute *text* to 300
characters and requires *language* to be populated for content in a
language other than English.

[[sec_8.6]]
=== issuance cycle

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# The cycle of issuing a product or
service.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*periodic date range* (see <<sec_8.8>>) +
*time interval of cycle* (see <<sec_8.19>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*issuance cycle* is encoded on *CatalogueElement* and records the pattern
by which a catalogued item is reissued. It works with *time interval of
cycle* and *periodic date range* to express both the length of the cycle
and the periods within it.

[[sec_8.7]]
=== online resource

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Information about online sources from
which a resource or data can be obtained.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*application profile* (see <<sec_7.4>>) +
*linkage* (see <<sec_7.47>>) +
*name of resource* (see <<sec_7.51>>) +
*online description* (see <<sec_7.55>>) +
*protocol* (see <<sec_7.68>>) +
*protocol request* (see <<sec_7.69>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*online resource* is encoded on *CatalogueElement*, *ContactDetails* and
*PriceInformation*. It identifies a network location from which a
resource may be obtained or at which further information is held. Where
several resources are available, each is encoded as a separate instance
rather than by combining addresses in a single *linkage*.

[[sec_8.8]]
=== periodic date range

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# The active period of a recurring event
or occurrence.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*date end* (see <<sec_7.23>>) +
*date start* (see <<sec_7.24>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*periodic date range* is encoded on *CatalogueElement*, and is used with
*issuance cycle* to express the periods during which a catalogued item is
issued or is applicable.

[[sec_8.9]]
=== pricing

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# A decision or establishment of a price.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*contract period* (see <<sec_7.19>>) +
*currency* (see <<sec_7.21>>) +
*price* (see <<sec_7.62>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*pricing* is encoded on *PriceInformation* and carries the commercial
terms. Both *currency* and *price* are mandatory within an instance of
it, as noted in the clause "Information types"; where an item is offered
on more than one set of terms, each is encoded as a separate instance of
*pricing*.

[[sec_8.10]]
=== print information

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Information on the printing of nautical
paper charts.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*print agency* (see <<sec_7.63>>) +
*print nation* (see <<sec_7.64>>) +
*reprint edition* (see <<sec_7.73>>) +
*reprint nation* (see <<sec_7.74>>) +
*print size* (see <<sec_8.11>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*print information* is encoded on *PhysicalProduct* and gathers the
characteristics of the printed sheet. It contains *print size*, which is
mandatory within it, and must therefore not be encoded unless the sheet
dimensions are known.

[[sec_8.11]]
=== print size

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Size of nautical paper charts.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*ISO 216* (see <<sec_7.42>>) +
*custom paper size* (see <<sec_8.2>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*print size* is encoded within *print information* on *PhysicalProduct*.
It expresses the sheet dimensions either as a standard size, using
*ISO 216*, or as explicit dimensions, using *custom paper size*.

[[sec_8.12]]
=== product specification

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# The name of the product specification
to which a nautical product adheres.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*edition date* (see <<sec_7.31>>) +
*ISSN* (see <<sec_7.43>>) +
*name* (see <<sec_7.50>>) +
*version* (see <<sec_7.92>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*product specification* is encoded on *ElectronicProduct* and on
*S100Service*. It identifies the specification governing the data. On
*S100Service* it must be distinguished from *service specification*,
which governs the service delivering that data; where both apply, both
are encoded.

[[sec_8.13]]
=== reference to NM

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# A reference to a specific Notice to
Mariners.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*publication date* (see <<sec_7.70>>) +
*week of year* (see <<sec_8.21>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* This attribute records the date of the last group of Notices to
Mariners consulted for the correction of the product, even where that
group, and possibly preceding groups, contained no updates to be applied
to the product in question.

* The value therefore states how far the product has been checked
against the Notices to Mariners, and not merely when it was last
altered.

|===

*reference to NM* is encoded on *PhysicalProduct* and records the
correction state of the printed product by reference to Notices to
Mariners. It contains *week of year*, whose sub-attributes are each
mandatory when it is encoded.

[[sec_8.14]]
=== service specification

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# The name of the (product) specification
to which a nautical service adheres.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*edition date* (see <<sec_7.31>>) +
*name* (see <<sec_7.50>>) +
*version* (see <<sec_7.92>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*service specification* is encoded on *S100Service* only. It identifies
the specification governing the service itself, as distinct from
*product specification*, which governs the data the service delivers.

[[sec_8.15]]
=== source indication

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Information about the source document,
publication, or reference from which object data or textual material
included or referenced in a dataset are derived.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*category of authority* (see <<sec_7.10>>) +
*country name* (see <<sec_7.20>>) +
*reported date* (see <<sec_7.72>>) +
*source* (see <<sec_7.77>>) +
*source type* (see <<sec_7.79>>) +
*feature name* (see <<sec_8.4>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*source indication* is encoded on *CatalogueElement*, *ContactDetails*
and *PriceInformation*. It records the provenance of the information
encoded, and its sub-attribute *source type* classifies the kind of
source from which that information was drawn.

[[sec_8.16]]
=== support file

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# Information on additional files used
in addition to nautical products.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*comment* (see <<sec_7.15>>) +
*digital signature reference* (see <<sec_7.26>>) +
*digital signature value* (see <<sec_7.27>>) +
*edition number* (see <<sec_7.32>>) +
*file locator* (see <<sec_7.34>>) +
*file name* (see <<sec_7.35>>) +
*issue date* (see <<sec_7.44>>) +
*other data type description* (see <<sec_7.58>>) +
*support file format* (see <<sec_7.81>>) +
*support file purpose* (see <<sec_7.82>>) +
*default locale* (see <<sec_8.3>>) +
*support file specification* (see <<sec_8.17>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*support file* is encoded on *CatalogueElement* and identifies an
external file accompanying the catalogued item. It is used with *support
file specification*, which identifies the format of that file.

[[sec_8.17]]
=== support file specification

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# The name of the product specification
to which a support file adheres.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*edition date* (see <<sec_7.31>>) +
*name* (see <<sec_7.50>>) +
*version* (see <<sec_7.92>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*support file specification* is encoded on *CatalogueElement* and states
the specification to which an associated support file conforms. A
support file whose specification is not identified cannot reliably be
interpreted by a consuming system, and the two should therefore be
encoded together.

[[sec_8.18]]
=== telecommunications

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# A means or channel of communicating
at a distance by electrical or electromagnetic means such as telegraphy,
telephony, or broadcasting.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*contact instructions* (see <<sec_7.18>>) +
*telecommunication identifier* (see <<sec_7.83>>) +
*telecommunication service* (see <<sec_7.84>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*telecommunications* is encoded on *ContactDetails* and carries a means
of contact other than a postal address. Each channel is encoded as a
separate instance, and within an instance the sub-attributes
*telecommunication identifier* and *telecommunication service* are
populated together, since an identifier cannot be interpreted without
knowing the service to which it applies.

[[sec_8.19]]
=== time interval of cycle

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# The temporal interval of the cycle
over which data is produced.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*type of time interval unit* (see <<sec_7.88>>) +
*value of time* (see <<sec_7.91>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*time interval of cycle* is encoded on *CatalogueElement* and expresses
the length of an issuance cycle. Its sub-attribute *type of time interval
unit* determines the unit in which the interval is expressed, and the
conventions for combining units are given in the clause "Attribute and
Enumerate Descriptions".

[[sec_8.20]]
=== time interval of product

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# The temporal interval over which the
product is updated or renewed.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*expiration date* (see <<sec_7.33>>) +
*issue date* (see <<sec_7.44>>) +
*issuance cycle* (see <<sec_8.6>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*time interval of product* is encoded on *CatalogueElement* and expresses
the period to which the catalogued item itself relates, as distinct from
*time interval of cycle*, which expresses the period at which it is
reissued.

[[sec_8.21]]
=== week of year

[cols=1,options="unnumbered"]
|===
a| [underline]#IHO Definition:# The indication of a specific week
within a specific year.

[underline]#Sub-attributes:#::
*week number* (see <<sec_7.94>>) +
*year number* (see <<sec_7.95>>)

[underline]#Remarks:#

* No remarks.

|===

*week of year* is encoded within *reference to NM* on *PhysicalProduct*.
Its sub-attributes *week number* and *year number* are each mandatory
when it is encoded, since a week number without a year does not identify
a particular Notice to Mariners.

This completes the description of the S-128 model, its attributes and
their permitted values.
